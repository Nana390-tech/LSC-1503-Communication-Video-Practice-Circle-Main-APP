🔥 Engage — Is your script ready?

Check in, then coach your teacher!

⏱ 10 min · Whole class. After the check-in, perform Ahmed's introduction badly (eyes down, fast, “umm”). Students tick what is missing, then you perform it again well.

1Script check-in

2What do I want to learn today?

3Coach's eye: coach your teacher!

Listen to your teacher read Ahmed's introduction. Use Cue Card 1. Tick everything that is missing or weak.

“Hi. So… my name is Ahmed. Umm… in this video I will talk about, like, some things. OK, let's start.”

🔍 Explore — Script health check

Check your own script with the cue cards, then swap and spot.

⏱ 15 min · Individual → pairs. 8 min self-check: students tick what their script has; the light turns green / amber / red. Then 5 min swap and spot with a partner.

1My script health check

Read your script one part at a time. Tick each item you already have.

🟢 everything is there · 🟡 one thing is missing · 🔴 two or more are missing

2Swap and spot

Swap scripts with a partner. They find ONE missing item in each part. Is your study skill one of the 7? Did you choose only one or two LPAs?

🧠 Explain — How a practice circle works

Three roles, helpful feedback and the 30-second huddle.

⏱ 15 min · Teacher-led. Hand out the Feedback Checklist (one per student). Model a huddle using the teacher's Engage introduction. Finish with the coach quiz.

1Three roles

2Feedback that helps

K
KindStart with a strength: “You did a great job with…”
S
SpecificSay which part: “In Part 2, I liked…”
H
HelpfulOne clear tip: “Next time, try to…”
🚫 Avoid: “It's good.” · “Bad.” · “I don't like it.” — they don't help the speaker improve.

3The 30-second huddle

1

2
Agree on ONE strengthThe best thing the speaker did.

Agree on ONE tipThe most important fix.

👥 Extend — Practise part by part

Speak · huddle · share · write your fix · rotate. About 8 minutes per part.

⏱ 40 min · Groups of three (or pairs). One device per group can run the timer. Coaches fill in the paper checklist for the speaker. Each student writes the feedback they received in their own app after their turn.

✅ Evaluate — Ready for Take One?

Rate your confidence, choose your top fixes and prove what you know.

⏱ 10 min · Individual. Confidence check, top 3 fixes, exit quiz (80% for the certificate) and exit ticket. Students print their practice report and save a backup.

1Confidence check

1 = not yet · 5 = ready to record

2My top 3 fixes before Take One

Tap a fix from your practice rounds, or write your own. Start with a verb: add, change, slow down…

3Exit quiz

6 questions. Score 80% or more for your Practice Circle certificate.

4Look back: did you learn it?
